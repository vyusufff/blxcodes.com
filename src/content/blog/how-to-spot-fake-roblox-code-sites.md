Searching “Roblox codes” pulls up real lists and a lot of junk. The junk is not always “wrong codes” — sometimes it is a trap for your password, Discord token, or credit card.

Here is a simple filter so you keep the free rewards and skip the scams.

## Red flags (leave immediately)

- **Free Robux generators** — there is no legit website that prints Robux from a code box on a random domain.
- **“Download this executor / .exe”** to unlock codes — malware bait.
- **Login with your Roblox password on a third-party site** — never. Use only roblox.com for account login.
- **Survey → survey → survey** walls before showing a single code.
- Pages that only show codes that “work” if you share to five friends or join a shady Discord with “free limiteds.”

If the pitch sounds like free money, it is not a codes page. It is a funnel.

## What a legit codes page looks like

- Codes are for a **named Roblox game**, not “all Robux.”
- **Active** and **expired** are separated.
- There is a clear **Updated** date when the list changed.
- Redeem happens **inside the game**, not by giving the site your password.
- No .exe, no “generator,” no “enter username to receive Robux.”

That is the standard we aim for on [BLXCodes](/codes): copy the string, redeem in-game, done.

## Quick safety checklist

1. Are you only looking up **game promo codes**?
2. Will you redeem **in the Roblox experience**, not on a random form?
3. Is anyone asking for your **password, cookie, or payment**?
4. Does the page push a **download** you did not ask for?

If step 3 or 4 is yes, close the tab.
