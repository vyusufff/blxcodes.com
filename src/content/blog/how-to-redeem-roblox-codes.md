Roblox game codes are usually short strings that unlock free rewards inside a specific experience — not always on the main Roblox website.

## Quick steps

1. Open the **game** that issued the code.
2. Find its **Codes** or Twitter icon in the UI.
3. Copy the code exactly (capitalization matters).
4. Redeem and check inventory / mailbox.

## Tips that save time

- Prefer pages that show **Last updated** and separate **active vs expired** codes.
- Redeem new codes early — popular ones expire or hit redeem limits.
- If a code fails, try again once, then assume it is expired for your account.
