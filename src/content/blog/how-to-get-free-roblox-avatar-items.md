You can dress an avatar for **0 Robux** if you know where Roblox hides freebies. What you cannot do is download a “generator” for free Robux — those are scams.

## 1. Filter the Avatar Shop for free items

1. Open the [Avatar Shop / Catalog](https://www.roblox.com/catalog).
2. Open **Filters**.
3. Set price to **0 – 0** Robux (or Free).
4. Sort by **Updated** or **Bestsellers** to find usable stuff fast.
5. Tap **Get** / **Buy** — it should cost nothing.

You’ll find shirts, pants, hats, faces, and accessories. Quality varies; try items on in the avatar editor before committing to a look.

## 2. Official Roblox promo codes (items, not Robux)

1. Go to Roblox’s official **Promo Codes** page while logged in.
2. Enter the code exactly.
3. Check your inventory / avatar.

Official codes unlock **avatar items or bundles**, not account Robux. Sites promising “Robux codes that always work” are lying or talking about **in-game** experience codes, which are different.

## 3. Events and partner experiences

Roblox and brands run limited-time experiences that award free accessories for playing or completing tasks. Enter only from:

- Roblox’s official event pages / homepage banners
- Verified brand partnerships announced by Roblox

Skip Discord links from strangers.

## Quick safety rules

- Never enter your password on a third-party “free item” site.
- Never install a random APK or browser extension for “free limiteds.”
- If it sounds unlimited and instant, it’s a scam.
