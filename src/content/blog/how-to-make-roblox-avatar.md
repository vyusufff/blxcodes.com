Your avatar is the first thing people see in experiences. You don’t need a huge Robux budget to look intentional.

## Open the avatar editor

1. Sign in to Roblox.
2. Tap the **Avatar** / character icon.
3. Use **Edit** to change body, clothing, accessories, and animations (if owned).

## Classic vs modern bodies

- **R6 / older classic** looks blocky and works in older games.
- **R15** supports more animations and layered clothing.
- Some experiences force a scale — your fancy layered fit may simplify in-game.

## Build a look on a budget

1. In the catalog, filter **price = 0** for free shirts, pants, and hats.
2. Pick **one** focal accessory (hat or back item) so the outfit isn’t noisy.
3. Match a simple color palette (2–3 colors).
4. Save multiple outfits if Roblox lets you — switch for games vs hanging out.

## Layered clothing tips

- Layered jackets/pants can clip on extreme body scales — preview in the editor.
- Hiding a classic shirt under layered pieces can reduce weird textures.
- Animations and emotes are separate purchases; they don’t change your clothes.

## Profile presentation

- Set a clear **display name**.
- Add an about blurb without Discord invite spam (that often looks scammy).
- Don’t wear stolen UGC copies from shady free model Discords — reports and moderation happen.
