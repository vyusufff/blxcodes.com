A verified email is how you recover passwords and get security alerts. Skip it and a locked account hurts more.

## Add / verify an email

1. Sign in → **Settings** → **Account Info**.
2. Add your email address.
3. Check the inbox for Roblox’s verification message.
4. Click the link (or enter the code if prompted).
5. Confirm the settings page shows the email as verified.

## Didn’t get the email?

- Wait a few minutes; hit **Resend**.
- Check spam / promotions.
- Make sure you typed the address correctly.
- School / work filters sometimes block Roblox — try a personal inbox.
- Sign out of other Roblox sessions that might be mid-change.

## “Email already in use”

That address is tied to another Roblox account. Recover that account, remove the email there, or use a different inbox. Don’t buy “email changers.”

## After verifying

- Enable **2FA**.
- Use an email password you don’t reuse on Roblox.
- Parents: keep the recovery email on an adult-controlled inbox for child accounts.

Verified email won’t stop every hack — pair it with a strong password and authenticator 2FA.
