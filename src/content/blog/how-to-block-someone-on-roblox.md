Blocking stops most contact from that user. Use it liberally for harassers — you’re not being rude; you’re setting a boundary.

## Block from a profile

1. Open their **profile**.
2. Tap the **⋯** menu.
3. Choose **Block**.
4. Confirm.

## Block in an experience

1. Open the player list / escape menu.
2. Select the player.
3. Choose **Block** (and **Report** if they broke rules).

## What blocking usually does

- Limits chat / messages from them (depending on systems).
- Stops friend requests and some join annoyances.
- Does **not** ban them from Roblox globally — only your interactions.

## Block vs report

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Annoying but not against rules | Block |
| Scam, hate, threats, NSFW | Report **and** block |
| Someone in your friend list turned toxic | Unfriend + block |

You can unblock later from settings / blocked users lists if you change your mind.
