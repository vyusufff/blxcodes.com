Deleting a Roblox account is permanent for that username’s content and purchases. If you only need a break, a privacy lockdown or logout is safer.

## Before you delete

- Spend or gift remaining Robux if you care (policies vary; assume they’re gone).
- Screenshot purchase history if you might need receipts.
- Leave groups / transfer game ownership if you’re a developer.
- Tell friends you’re leaving so they don’t think you were hacked.

## Official deletion path

1. Sign in on the Roblox website.
2. Open **Settings** → look for **Account** / **Parental** / **Delete** options (labels change).
3. Follow Roblox’s **Delete Account** / GDPR-style request flow for your region.
4. Confirm with email / password when asked.

If you can’t find the button, search Roblox Help for “delete account” and use the linked official article — not a YouTube description link.

## Under 13 / child accounts

A parent or guardian usually must complete deletion through parental controls or support. Don’t share the child’s password in random Discords.

## Softer alternatives

- Make the account **private** and clear social settings.
- Remove payment methods.
- Simply stop playing — dormant accounts don’t need deletion.

Once deleted, you generally **cannot** reclaim the same account. Choose carefully.
