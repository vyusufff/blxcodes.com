Roblox trading is only for **Limited** and **Limited U** items — not normal clothing you bought yesterday. Both sides usually need **Roblox Premium**, a qualifying account age, and trading turned on.

## Requirements checklist

- Roblox **Premium** active on both accounts (typical rule).
- Account age / eligibility meets Roblox’s trade rules.
- **Privacy** → trading allowed (not set to No One).
- Items must be marked **Limited** or **Limited U**.

## How to send a trade

1. Open the other player’s **profile**.
2. Use the menu (⋯) → **Trade**.
3. Put up your Limited items and optional Robux (if the UI allows).
4. Review their offer carefully.
5. Send. They accept, decline, or counter.

You can also manage pending trades from the **Trades** page in the Roblox website menu.

## Value basics (don’t wing it)

- Check recent sales / demand on trusted value trackers before accepting.
- A “W / L / F” (win / loss / fair) opinion is not a Roblox guarantee — it’s community math.
- Brand-new Limiteds swing hard in the first days.

## Common scams

- **“Trust trade”** — they ask you to give items first “then I’ll add.” Never.
- **Fake middleman** — random Discord mods are not Roblox staff.
- **Item switching** — review the final trade window right before accept.
- **Off-platform deals** — PayPal / crypto for Roblox items breaks TOS and is easy to reverse-scam.

If a deal only works in DMs and feels rushed, walk away.
