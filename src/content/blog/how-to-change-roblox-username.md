Roblox gives you two name layers: a **display name** (what friends see in-game) and a **username** (your @handle / login identity). Most players only need a display-name change. Username changes cost Robux and have limits.

## Display name (free, quick)

1. Open Roblox on the web or app and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings** → **Account Info** (or your avatar settings on mobile).
3. Edit **Display Name**.
4. Save. Display names can usually be changed again after a short cooldown.

Tips:

- Keep it readable — spammy symbols make you look like a bot.
- Display names are **not** unique; many people can share the same one.
- Your real username still appears on your profile URL.

## Username (costs Robux)

1. On the Roblox website, open **Settings** → **Account Info**.
2. Choose **Birthday** / account section where **Username** can be changed (wording varies by app version).
3. Enter a new username that is available.
4. Confirm the Robux charge.

Rules that catch people off guard:

- Usernames must be unique and follow Roblox naming rules.
- There is a **cooldown** between username changes.
- Old usernames can be claimed by someone else later — don’t expect to “save” them forever.
- If you use the same password elsewhere, change it after a public rename so impersonators can’t socially engineer friends.

## Can’t change it?

- Not enough Robux for a username change.
- Name taken, banned word, or too similar to a restricted brand.
- Account under moderation or pending verification.
- You’re looking in the mobile app UI — try **roblox.com** in a browser.

## Stay safe after renaming

Scammers sometimes pretend to be “Roblox Support” after you rename. Roblox staff will never ask for your password or 2FA codes in chat.
