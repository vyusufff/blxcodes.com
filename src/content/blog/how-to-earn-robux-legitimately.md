## Before you start

- **Never** share your Roblox password, cookie, or 2FA codes.
- Roblox does **not** give free Robux through random websites or Discord “generators.”
- Gift cards and payouts depend on your **country** and account eligibility.
- For free *in-game* rewards (not account Robux), use verified [game codes](/codes) instead.

---

<h2 id="microsoft-rewards">1. Microsoft Rewards</h2>

[Microsoft Rewards](https://rewards.microsoft.com/) lets you earn points by searching with Bing, completing daily sets, and finishing simple quizzes. In many regions you can redeem those points for a **Roblox gift card**, then apply it on Roblox.

### How it usually works

1. Create or sign in to a Microsoft account and join Microsoft Rewards.
2. Earn points (daily sets, searches, offers — rules vary by country).
3. Open the Rewards redeem catalog and look for a **Roblox** gift card.
4. Redeem, copy the code, and add it on Roblox via the official gift-card / redeem flow.
5. Robux appear after the card processes (usually quickly).

### Tips

- Confirm Roblox cards appear in **your** country’s catalog before you grind points.
- Treat Rewards like a slow drip — not overnight thousands of Robux.
- Use only official Microsoft / Bing Rewards flows — not “Rewards bots.”

**Best for:** beginners who want Robux without creating anything.

<h2 id="make-clothing">2. Make and sell clothing</h2>

Classic **shirts, pants, and T-shirts** are a common starter creator path. You design on Roblox clothing templates, upload them, set a Robux price, and earn a creator share when someone buys (Roblox takes a marketplace fee).

### Basic flow

1. Learn Roblox clothing template sizes (shirt/pants layouts matter).
2. Design in any image editor; keep textures clean in-game.
3. Upload from Creator Dashboard / avatar upload tools.
4. Price competitively and use a clear name + honest thumbnail.
5. Share items on socials, groups, or your own game.

### Tips

- Start simple and trendy before ultra-complex art.
- Follow copyright / trademark / content rules before uploading.
- Sales are uneven — one viral piece can beat twenty ignored uploads.

**Best for:** players who like design more than coding.

<h2 id="create-ugc">3. Create UGC (accessories)</h2>

**UGC** means 3D avatar items — hats, faces, accessories, and other marketplace creations. More technical than flat clothing, but the upside can be larger if an item trends.

### What you need

- Comfort with 3D modeling (or a starter tool you can learn).
- Meeting Roblox’s current Creator / marketplace requirements for your region.
- Time to iterate: mesh, textures, thumbnails, and pricing.

### Realistic expectations

- Quality and trends beat upload spam.
- Fees and eligibility change — read the latest Creator docs on Roblox.
- Copying popular limiteds or branded IP gets moderated fast.

**Best for:** creators ready to learn 3D and treat Roblox like a small product business.

---

<h2 id="make-games">4. Make games</h2>

Building experiences is the long-term path: **Game Passes**, **Developer Products**, engagement payouts when eligible, and **DevEx** if you meet Roblox’s cash-out rules.

### How games generate Robux

- **Game Passes** — permanent unlocks (VIP, cosmetics, tools).
- **Developer Products** — consumables (coins, spins, lives).
- **Engagement / Premium-related payouts** — when eligible players spend time in your place (details set by Roblox and can change).
- **Official creator / affiliate tools only** — ignore shady “traffic for Robux” middlemen.

### Honest timeline

A polished game takes weeks or months. Most new places earn little until retention and monetization click. Treat it like a product, not a free-Robux button.

**Best for:** builders and teams who enjoy iteration.

## What does *not* count

Avoid completely:

- “Free Robux” generators and random browser extensions
- Surveys or apps that ask you to log in on a non-official site
- Anyone offering Robux if you “go first” or share account access
- Stolen card codes or Discord “limited stock” scams

If it promises thousands of Robux for one click, it is not legitimate.
