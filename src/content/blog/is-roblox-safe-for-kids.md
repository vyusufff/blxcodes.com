Roblox can be fine for kids **with supervision and the right account settings**. It is also a huge social platform — treat it more like moderated multiplayer than a single-player toy.

## Real risks (not myths)

- Inappropriate chat or voice in open experiences.
- Scams: fake free Robux, phishing links, “trust trades.”
- User-generated experiences vary wildly in quality and age-appropriateness.
- Oversharing personal info (school, address, photos).

## Settings that actually help

1. Create / convert to an age-appropriate account with parental controls.
2. Restrict who can message and friend your child.
3. Turn on account PIN / parent lock where available.
4. Disable voice chat unless you approve it.
5. Review recently played experiences together.

## Healthy habits

- Play in the same room sometimes; ask what games they like.
- Agree on rules: no password sharing, no off-platform payments to strangers.
- Report + block is normal — teach the buttons.
- Prefer well-known experiences for younger kids.

Roblox invests in safety tools, but **parents + privacy settings** do most of the work. Panic bans (“never play online games”) usually fail; clear rules work better.
