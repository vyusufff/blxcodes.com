Voice chat lets you talk in supported experiences. Roblox gates it behind age / ID verification in many regions.

## Typical enable steps

1. Sign in on the Roblox **website** (verification is often web-first).
2. Open **Settings** → **Voice** / **Privacy** sections.
3. Complete any **age verification** Roblox requires for your country.
4. Allow microphone permission on your device / browser.
5. Join a voice-enabled experience and use the in-game mute / voice UI.

If the toggle is missing, your account type, age setting, or region may not qualify yet.

## In-game controls

- Mute yourself or others when needed.
- Leave voice channels if harassment starts — then report.
- Headphones reduce echo on desktop.

## Safety notes

- Don’t share real name, school, or location on voice.
- Parents: keep voice **off** for younger kids unless you’re present.
- Voice scams exist (“go to this site for free Robux”) — hang up and report.

Voice is optional. Many top experiences play fine without it.
