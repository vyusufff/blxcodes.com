Locked out of Roblox? Most recoveries work through the official reset flow — not Discord “helpers.”

## If you still know the email or username

1. Go to Roblox → **Forgot password?** / login help.
2. Enter the email or username on the account.
3. Open the reset email (check spam).
4. Set a **new unique password**.
5. Sign in and enable **2-Step Verification** immediately.

## If the email was changed by a hacker

1. Use Roblox’s official **account recovery** / support forms only from roblox.com or the Help Center.
2. Provide proof you own the account (old passwords, billing, gift card receipts, creation date, friends list details).
3. Never send passwords or authenticator codes to strangers.

## After you get back in

- Change password + enable authenticator 2FA.
- Check connected devices / sessions and sign out unknowns.
- Review email on the account; secure that inbox too.
- Warn friends: your account may have messaged spam while compromised.

## Scams to ignore

- “Pay me Robux / crypto and I’ll unlock it.”
- Fake Roblox login pages from chat links.
- “Download this unlocker EXE.”

If recovery fails, document everything and retry the official form with clearer ownership proof.
