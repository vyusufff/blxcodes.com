Reporting works best when you give Roblox a clear category and evidence. Rage-reporting after a fair death in a fighting game usually goes nowhere.

## Report in an experience

1. Open the player list / escape menu (varies by device).
2. Select the player → **Report**.
3. Choose the closest category (cheating, bullying, inappropriate chat, etc.).
4. Add a short, factual note if the form allows.
5. Submit. Don’t confront them in chat afterward.

## Report a user from their profile

1. Open their **profile**.
2. Use the **⋯** menu → **Report**.
3. Pick the reason and submit.

## Report chat or messages

Abusive DMs and group chat can usually be reported from the message menu. Keep screenshots for your own records; Roblox’s systems also capture context when you use the official report button.

## What happens next

- You often **won’t** get a public update.
- Roblox may warn, ban, or take no action if rules weren’t broken.
- False reporting can get *your* account in trouble.

## Safety extras

- Block the player after reporting if they’re harassing you.
- Turn on **2FA** so revenge hack attempts are harder.
- Never share personal info (school, address, phone) in chat.
- Parents: use parental controls / account restrictions for younger users.
