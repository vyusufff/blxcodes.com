Roblox Premium is a monthly subscription. You pay real money; Roblox pays back a **Robux stipend** and unlocks features free accounts don’t get.

## What you usually get

- Monthly **Robux stipend** (amount depends on tier).
- Ability to **trade** Limited items (when other rules are met).
- Selling / payout features tied to Premium for creators in many cases.
- Premium-only perks in some experiences (badges, doors, multipliers — game-dependent).
- Slight profile flair (Premium icon).

Exact stipend numbers change; check Roblox’s Premium page for your country before buying.

## Who it’s worth it for

**Yes, consider it if you:**

- Trade Limiteds regularly.
- Sell clothing / UGC and need Premium-gated features.
- Already spend more than one stipend’s value on Robux each month (subscription can be cleaner than random packs).

**Skip it if you:**

- Only play free games and never buy avatar items.
- Are saving for one small purchase — a single Robux pack may be enough.
- Are a young player without parent approval.

## Premium vs buying Robux once

| Goal | Better pick |
| --- | --- |
| One avatar item | One-time Robux |
| Trading hobby | Premium |
| Making & selling items | Premium (usually) |
| Just chatting / obbies | Free is fine |

## Avoid Premium scams

Nobody can “gift Premium” through a sketchy link. Buy only inside Roblox or official storefronts. Cancel anytime in your Microsoft / Apple / Google / Roblox billing settings so it doesn’t renew by surprise.
