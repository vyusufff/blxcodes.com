Playing together is the whole point for many people. Roblox makes joins easy when privacy settings allow it.

## Join from the Friends page

1. Open **Friends** / **Connections**.
2. Find a friend who is **In Experience**.
3. Click **Join** / the game thumbnail.
4. Roblox launches the same experience (server may or may not be identical depending on game).

## Join from their profile

Open the friend’s profile while they’re online → use **Join** if shown.

## Join failed — common causes

- Their privacy blocks follows / joins.
- The server filled up between click and launch.
- The experience disabled join-from-friends.
- Platform mismatch / update required.
- You’re blocked.

Ask them for a **private server** link or to create a party/queue inside the game if it has one.

## Tips that save time

- Everyone update Roblox before a group session.
- Use a VIP/private server for tryouts and content recording.
- Agree on a backup game if the main one is broken.

If joins keep failing, compare privacy settings on both accounts — “Friends” on invites / follows is the sweet spot for most groups.
