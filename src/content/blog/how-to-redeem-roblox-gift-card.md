Gift cards are the safest way someone else can buy you Robux. Redeem on the **correct account** — Robux do not move between users after redeem.

## Redeem on the website (clearest path)

1. Sign in at [roblox.com](https://www.roblox.com/) on the account that should receive Robux.
2. Open the **Robux** menu → **Redeem** (or go to the gift-card redeem page).
3. Enter the PIN exactly (no spaces unless shown on the card).
4. Confirm. Your Robux balance updates when the code is accepted.

## Redeem on mobile

1. Open the Roblox app and sign in.
2. Open the Robux / purchase area.
3. Look for **Redeem Gift Card** (wording varies by iOS / Android store rules).
4. If the app won’t show redeem, use a browser → roblox.com instead.

## Common errors

- **Already redeemed** — pin was used or leaked from a photo of the card.
- **Wrong region** — some cards are country-locked.
- **Typos** — `0` vs `O`, missing characters.
- **Wrong account** — you redeemed while logged into a sibling’s profile.

## Tips

- Scratch gently; damaged pins are harder to support.
- Don’t post the full PIN in screenshots or Discord.
- Keep the receipt until Robux appear.
- Parents: redeem yourself if kids share devices, so Robux land on the intended account.
