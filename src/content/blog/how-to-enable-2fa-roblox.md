Two-step verification (2FA) is the highest-impact security setting on Roblox. If someone steals your password, they still need a code from your phone.

## Turn 2FA on

1. Sign in at [roblox.com](https://www.roblox.com/).
2. Open the gear → **Settings**.
3. Open **Security**.
4. Enable **2-Step Verification**.
5. Prefer an **authenticator app** (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy) over SMS when both are offered.
6. Scan the QR code, enter the 6-digit code, and save your **recovery / backup codes** offline.

## Why authenticator beats SMS

- SIM-swap attacks can steal SMS codes.
- Authenticator codes work offline.
- Backup codes let you recover if you replace your phone.

## After you enable it

- Never share 2FA codes in Discord, “Roblox support” DMs, or “verification” websites.
- Add a recovery email you control.
- If Roblox asks for a code at login, open your authenticator app — not a random link from chat.

## Lost your authenticator?

1. Try backup codes you saved when enabling 2FA.
2. Use Roblox account recovery with proof of ownership (email, billing, account details).
3. Do **not** pay strangers who claim they can “unlock” accounts.

Enabling 2FA takes a few minutes and blocks most casual account thefts.
