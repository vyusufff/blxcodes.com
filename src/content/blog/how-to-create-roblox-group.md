Groups are how clans, studios, and friend circles organize on Roblox. Creating one is straightforward; running one well takes a bit of setup.

## Create the group

1. Sign in on the Roblox **website**.
2. Open the **Groups** section.
3. Choose **Create Group**.
4. Pay the one-time Robux creation fee (shown in the UI).
5. Set a name, description, and icon.

Pick a name you can live with — renames aren’t always free or instant.

## Set roles (ranks)

Default ranks are rarely enough. Add roles like:

- Owner / Admin
- Developer / Builder
- Moderator
- Member
- Guest / Ally (optional)

For each role, toggle permissions carefully: who can spend group funds, shout, accept joins, or manage games.

## Useful settings

- **Entry**: public join vs request-only.
- **Shout**: announcements members see on the group page.
- **Games**: attach experiences your community plays.
- **Funds**: only give spend permission to people you trust.

## Growing without spam

- Share the group link in your game’s description.
- Host events in your experience; don’t mass-DM strangers.
- Write clear rules (no scams, no harassment).
- Remove bots that join only to advertise.

If the goal is a developer studio, keep the owner account secured with 2FA — group ownership disputes are painful.
